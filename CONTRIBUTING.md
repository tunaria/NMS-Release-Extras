royalty-free, irrevocable licence to host, run, modify, adapt, publish, and distribute your
contributions — including in this project's public code repositories, and including after you
stop contributing. Others may then use your work under whatever licence that repository carries.

**No payment, ever.** This is a free educational space. Contributing never earns money,
royalties, or compensation of any kind, now or later.
